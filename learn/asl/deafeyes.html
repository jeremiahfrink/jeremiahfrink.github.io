What this page is

You already started Through Deaf Eyes, the PBS documentary about 200 years of Deaf life in America. This page catches your notes while you keep watching.

  • Watch for about 30 minutes, starting where you stopped.
  • Pause the film whenever something matters and write it down in the right section.
  • You do not need to fill every box. You need enough notes that you could explain what you watched to someone else.
  • Your answers save on this computer as you type. When you finish, press Copy as Markdown at the bottom and paste it into your Deaf Culture Notebook.

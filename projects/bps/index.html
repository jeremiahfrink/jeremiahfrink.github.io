What you'll present

Your group will do two things:

1. Upload your artifact to the shared Drive folder using the Share Your Artifact button below. Name your file GroupName_ProjectTitle. Upload your Report-Out Card as a second file.

2. Give a two-minute report-out answering:

  • What we created.
  • Who it helps.
  • How AI strengthened the work.
  • What we deliberately kept human, social, physical, or offline.
  • What we learned.
  • What we need in order to be successful this year.

This page builds your Report-Out Card for you at the final step from what you enter along the way.

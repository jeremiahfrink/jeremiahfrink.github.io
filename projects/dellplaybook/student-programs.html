Signature Programs

Student Programs

Four free programs districts can adopt. Real student experiences, not marketing plays: they open doors, deepen relationships, and give attendees and leaders something to take home beyond product.

The fastest way in: know which program fits the conversation you're in, share the one-pager, and loop in your strategist to take it from interest to a running program.

Girls Who Game

Extracurricular · Open to all students

STEM and leadership through game-based learning in Minecraft Education. Builds computational thinking, collaboration, and confidence, with educator coaching and mentor connections included.

Created by Dell with Microsoft and Intel

One-pager (PDF)

Student TechCrew

Ages 13-18 · Grant-based, free

Student-run tech help desks. Students repair, update, and troubleshoot the district's own devices, build IT and service skills, and can earn Dell TechDirect industry credentials while the district gains real support capacity.

Dell Technologies · studenttechcrew.com

One-pager (PDF)

Data Dunkers

Grades 5-12 · Classroom, after-school, or summer

Data science and AI literacy through basketball statistics. Students learn to ask questions, gather and clean data, analyze, and present insights, with ready-to-use activities for educators.

Partnership with the PS43 Foundation

One-pager (PDF)

Tech Career Circuit

Grades 6-12 · Free educator hub

Ready-made, standards-aligned lessons preparing students for in-demand IT careers: workforce readiness, hardware, leadership, customer service, and career exploration, all LMS-compatible.

Launched by Dell with Discovery Education

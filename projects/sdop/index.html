What we can and cannot use

The question that comes up most
Our license
Google Workspace for Education Standard. We do not have Google AI Pro for Education, Education Plus, or the Teaching and Learning add-on, so a good deal of what Google announces does not reach us.
Gemini and Gemini Notebook
All staff. Students in grades 9 to 12 only. Below grade 9 the work happens on the teacher's side and students see the product.
Gems
Available to staff, and shareable with colleagues.
Adobe Express with Firefly
Open to students at every grade we serve. For most elementary and middle rooms this is the student creation tool.
Turned off here
Google Chat, and most of Google Meet.

Between now and September 25

1. Do one thing, with one real teacher

The scenario you picked at a station. Not a pilot, not a rollout. Come back ready to say which teacher, what you shared, what happened, and what students could do. Dead ends count, so bring those too.

2. Confirm what we actually have in Classroom

Open the Gemini tab in one of your classes, note what you can see, and add it to the changes sheet. Five minutes each, and across twenty one of us it produces a verified list none of us could build alone.
